# Template writing style

Use clear, specific English in the example interface. Keep placeholders visibly generic. Do not present fictional clients, credentials, awards, education, dates, usage figures, or personal experience as real facts.

Explain what a component does and what users can change. Distinguish functioning demos from placeholders and future ideas. The configurator uses demonstration estimates, not commercial quotations. Media without a source remains a placeholder.

Keep private portfolio material out of code, metadata, alt text, links, documentation, screenshots, and embedded images. Review new assets as well as text before publishing.
