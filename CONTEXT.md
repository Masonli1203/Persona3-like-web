# Creative browsing

The vocabulary for browsing creative works and photo series in the portfolio.

## Language

**Creative work**:
A film, visual experiment, or photo series presented within a creative category.
_Avoid_: Case study (a separate kind of portfolio project).

**Photo series**:
A creative work made up of an ordered collection of photographs.
_Avoid_: Gallery (ambiguous between a collection and its presentation).

**Viewing session**:
A visit to an open creative work, including browsing its photo series and returning to the works list.
_Avoid_: Page session (which may contain several viewing sessions).

**Series position**:
The place a visitor reached in the photo series overview before opening a photograph or closing the work.
_Avoid_: Current photo (the photograph being viewed at full size).
